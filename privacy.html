Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy draft explains the information inBestmen expects to use to provide accounts, project discovery and community participation.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, this may include your name, email address, account identifier, project content, support activity and technical information needed to keep the service secure.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, show dashboards, process authorized actions, improve project review and communicate important service updates. We do not place private API keys in browser code.

Third-party services

Firebase may provide authentication and database services, PayPal may process payments when enabled, and ElevenLabs may provide project narration or conversational agents. Each service has its own terms and privacy documentation.

Data choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your account information by contacting hello@inbestmen.com, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Important launch note

This is a product-stage draft and must be reviewed for the jurisdictions where inBestmen operates before collecting real financial or identity data.
