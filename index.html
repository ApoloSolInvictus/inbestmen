The iB participation layer

One simple coin. A world of useful support.

iB Coins make community support visible, measurable and easy to understand. Every approved project shows its goal, its progress and the signal from the people behind it.

Platform value1 iB Coin = $1 USD

One iB Coin represents one US dollar of participation inside inBestmen.

iB Coins are purchased through PayPal and held in a personal wallet until the member chooses an approved project. They are a platform participation unit, not a cryptocurrency and not a promise of financial return. Final payment and legal terms apply before launch.

One planet, many voices

The first conversation can happen in your language.

Our ElevenLabs intake agent welcomes founders through Telegram and the web, asks the same essential questions and listens for the details a human review team needs. Participants choose their preferred language before the conversation begins.

That conversation is organized into a structured project brief. The agent does not approve or fund an idea by itself: it prepares a clear signal for human judgment, while the community decides where support should gather.
